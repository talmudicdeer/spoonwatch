## Spoonwatch

### Winners of the Wooden Spoon: Ottawa Redblacks (0-11)

Yep. Just because you were on a bye doesn't mean the nightmare is over. Buckle up, because Toronto's next.

### Everyone Else

**Hamilton Tiger Cats (4-9):** That LDC couldn't have gone a whole lot worse. Horrendous loss to the Argos and an extremely scary head injury for Tre Ford. The Ticats now have to win out to avoid getting crossed over by, quite literally, the entire West. All of that with the league's 8th-ranked defense and an offense that keeps getting closer and closer to Ottawa's basement futon with each passing week. Not great!

**Winnipeg Blue Bombers (6-6):** Things were going great, until they didn't. There's going to be a lot of finger-pointing at why Winnipeg lost this game. The Banjo Bowl is now an absolute must-win to keep their playoff hopes alive, both for the third West spot and third East spot.

**Toronto Argonauts (6-6):** Things are looking up for Toronto. A big win over Hamilton is followed up by an ostensible cakewalk against Ottawa, which would make their #2 spot in the East that much more secure.
